Share your signal live

Everyone in the room streams their raw EMG signal. Your page runs each signal through your filters, FFT and trained posture model to decide who is standing or lying. Nobody sets a status by hand.
Your model learned from your body. It works best on other people when their electrodes and amplifier gain match yours. If their results look wrong, they can record a little data and train their own model.
This uses a free public MQTT server. Anyone with the room code can receive the signals in it, so keep the random code and use just a first name.

Safety alerts

Alerts run on this computer for you and for everyone streaming in the room, using your model. Calf EMG looks the same when sitting, lying or fallen, so sitting down quickly can also set off a fall alert. Adding an accelerometer would make fall detection dependable. This is a student project, not a medical device.

Muscle fatigue test

Stand on tiptoes (a calf raise) and hold it as long as you can, then press Stop. As a muscle tires, the median frequency of its EMG falls. The slope of that fall is the fatigue index. You can test anyone who is streaming in the room.

Travel journal

How to play

Keyboard: ← / → or A / D turn · ↑ / ↓ or W / S climb / dive · Space boost (race) or flap (travel) · B makes your bird call (hold for long calls, Shift / Ctrl change the pitch, or click the view)
Eyes: look left or right of the centre to turn, above or below it to climb or dive. Look at the middle to fly straight.
Flex your calf: harder flex = faster. In a race it boosts; in travel it flaps (relax to glide).
C : change camera · H : hide the HUD · P : take a photo
Boosting drains stamina (the thin bar). Relax to refill it.
Rocket start: hold a 45–75% flex when GO appears. Flexing too hard stalls you.
Golden rings: updraft, extra speed and stamina.
Dodge floating rocks, balloons, turbine blades and pillars. Storm clouds slow you down.
R : back onto the course
No timer, no rivals. Fly anywhere and find the hidden places (gold ◆ on the compass and map).
Diving gains speed and climbing costs speed, like a real bird. Shimmering columns are warm rising air: circle in them to climb without flapping.

1 · Start eye tracking

Uses your laptop webcam. The video is processed in this browser and never uploaded. The first start downloads the tracker and its face models (a few MB).

2 · Calibrate

Sit the way you normally will, keep your head fairly still and light your face from the front. Look straight at each glowing dot and tap your foot (or click the dot) 3 times, and keep looking at it for a moment after each tap. A bright screen and a lamp in front of your face both help. Calibration is saved for next time.

3 · Control

Eye clicks work on every tab except Tap Brawl, where a foot tap attacks. A web page can't move the Windows mouse pointer, so eye control works inside this page only.
